## Research summary (detail 02-script.md ke Sources me)
- Purane ChatGPT Plugins (2023) **9 April 2024 ko band** ho gaye; unki jagah pehle GPTs, phir Connectors→Apps aaye, aur July 2026 se ye sab **Plugin Directory / Settings → Plugins** ke naam se milta hai.
- Connect flow (sab sources pe ek jaisa): **Settings → Plugins (Apps) → app chuno (Gmail/Google Drive/Google Calendar) → Connect → Google sign-in → permissions review karke Allow** → chat me @mention ya + se use, ya relevant sawal pe ChatGPT khud reference karta hai.
- Google apps: **Gmail** (mail padhna, summary, reply draft), **Google Drive** (Docs/Sheets/Slides search + summarize/edit), **Google Calendar** (schedule padhna, event banana), Contacts. Kai paid plans (Plus/Pro/Business) pe; kai Google accounts ek saath connect support bhi aa gaya hai.
