# NARRATED WALKTHROUGH SCRIPT — P001: ChatGPT × Google Connect (Hinglish)

**Format:** 50s video (music + SFX, koi voiceover nahi) ke saath bolne wala walkthrough. Narration video ke text ko dohraayegi nahi — uske aas-paas ki baat karegi. “⏸” par video roko.
**Voice:** faisla baad me; script ready hai. Kul narration ~90 second ki hai.

---

[0:00 · Hook ke saath]

“Pehle chaar second me video apna sabse bada daava kar deta hai: ChatGPT ab tumhara Gmail bhi padhega. Dhyaan do — tareeka baad me aayega, fayda pehle. Aur ye sab bina bole ho raha hai; screen ka text hi is video ki awaz hai.”

[0:04.5 · Plugins wale hisse par ⏸]

“Yahan ek sach saaf kiya gaya hai: purane Plugins band ho chuke hain. Agar tum purani jaankari lekar aaye the, to video tumhe ladta nahi — raasta dikhata hai: Settings, phir Plugins. Menu ke naam samay ke saath badal sakte hain, lekin video ka usool wahi hai — hamesha current official raaste se jao.”

[0:10 · 4 steps chalao]

“Connect karna chaar steps me toda gaya hai: Settings kholo, Plugins ya Apps, Gmail chuno, Connect karke Google par Allow. Sirf chaar. Agar koi tutorial isse zyada lamba lage, to samjho wahi phasa raha hai.”

[0:22 · Gmail examples par ⏸]

“Judne ke baad kya hoga, ye video do roz-wale sawalon se dikhata hai: aaj ke important mail batao, aur iska reply draft karo. Koi fancy demo nahi — wahi kaam jo tum roz karte ho.”

[0:31 · Drive/Calendar chalao]

“Wahi pattern Drive aur Calendar par dohraya gaya hai: dhundo, samjho, kaam karo. Report ki summary ho, ya kal ka din kaisa hai — assistant har jagah ek hi tareeke se kaam karta hai.”

[0:41 · Permission wali line par ⏸ — yahan zyada ruko]

“Aur ye hai poori video ki sabse zimmedari wali line: permission padh ke Allow karo. Connect karna matlab kisi app ko apne data tak pahunch dena hai. Allow dabane se pehle do second ruk kar padhna — yahi is video ki asli seekh hai. Aur pasand na aaye to disconnect kabhi bhi, Settings se.”

[0:46 · Final hold]

“Aakhri line video ko band karti hai: ChatGPT sirf chat nahi, tumhara assistant hai. Aur ‘Save kar lo’ isliye kaam karta hai kyunki aakhri frame rukta hai — tumhe sochne ka samay milta hai. Pehle Gmail try karo — baaki phir.”

---

Recording: video ka music pehle se hai (−14.3 LUFS); narration uske upar halki si jagah bana kar baithe — music ko har narration line par thoda neeche (duck) karna hoga. On-screen text bilkul mat badlo.
