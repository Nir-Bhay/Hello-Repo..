# Series Bible — RACTOR (docs/45 · T164)

Har episode naye sire se na soche, uske liye series ke sthaayi niyam ek jagah. Episode-specific baatein (kahani, shabdon ki fehrist, forbidden states) har episode ke apne Director's Note me hongi — ye page un sab ke *upar* ke niyam hai.

## Duniya aur palette (taala laga hua)

- Paper `#F4F1E9` · near-white `#FFFDFA` · ink `#142B3A`
- Blue `#42698D` · pale blue `#A6BFD2` · muted `#66727C`
- Red `#B34431` · pale red `#F1E1DC` (khatra/bahari asar)
- Green `#48765E` · pale green `#E3ECE5` (green **sirf** boundary hardware par — handles, shutter, restrict-frame)
- Ochre `#B09164` (chetavni/“Next:” jaise kinare wale istemal) · plum `#715767` · skin `#BF8C70`

## Copy ke niyam

- On-screen text chhota, par **exact** — script hi copy-ledger hai; render ke baad koi shabd nahi badalta bina naya version numer.
- Aankh se padhne layak samay har copy-card ko mile (frame-cues par legit “padhne layak/settled” pal).
- Jhoothi safai mana hai: “test passed”, “sab safe hai” jaise daave kabhi bina saboot ke nahi. Kahani fail-copy hi istemal karti hai jab sach yahi ho.

## Camera aur harkat

- Teen camera holds ka siddhant (wide / desk / boundary). Frame ka dhyaan ek — **focal owner rule**: har frame par ek hi cheez sabse zyada cheekhti hai.
- Harkat me bhaar (weight) ho: overshoot/springy “cartoon bounce” nahi; anticipation chhota, follow-through imaandar.
- Pehle **sabse mushkil harkat ka proof**, phir poora render. 14→19 anchors wali contact sheet par review lazmi.

## Forbidden states (har episode)

- Koi bhi “chal raha hai” wala sanket uske asal pal se pehle nahi (RUN se pehle RUNNING kabhi nahi).
- Jo cheez jahan hai wahi rahe jab tak kahani use hilaye nahi (K01 jagah par; token bahar gaya).
- Aakhri frame apni series ka sach bole: jo episode bana nahi, uska vaada sirf “Next:” tak — kahani gadhkar daava nahi.

## Awaaz (jab aaye)

- Narration scripts pehle se likhe milte hain (har production ke folder me). Recording ke baad SRT, phir −14 LUFS par mix, phir phone test.
- Silent version kabhi hataaya nahi jayega: naya version, naya naam (docs/37).

## Har episode ki lazmi saath-wali cheezein

Walkthrough + transcript (docs/17 index me kadhi), cover (docs/28 ke niyam), review anchors, aur `docs/33` me entry. Kuch bhi “final” tabhi jab **frame-saboot** ke saath verify ho chuka ho.
