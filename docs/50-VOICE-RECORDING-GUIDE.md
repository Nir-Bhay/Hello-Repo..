# Voice Recording Guide — Agar tum "voice: own" chuno (T126 ki taiyaari)

Scripts pehle se tayyaar hain aur frame-time ke hisaab se likhe gaye hain. Tumhara kaam sirf padhna hai; edit, saafai, SRT aur mix mera kaam hoga.

## Jagah aur setup (5 minute)

- **Band kamra**, pankha/AC band, khidki band. Kambal wala kona sabse accha reverb-killer hai.
- Phone/laptop mic **munh se 15–20 cm**, thoda sa side me (seedha saamne "ph" ki phoonk lagegi).
- Earphones pehno taaki koi guide-audio record na ho; bolne ki speed **shant aur saaf** — radio wali tezi nahi.

## Recording ka tareeka

1. Ek baithak me **ek script** — pehle ASI05 Hinglish (productions/002.../08), phir English (07), phir P001 (productions/001.../06).
2. Har line ke beech **2 second ki khamoshi** — edit me wahi kaam aayegi.
3. Galti ho to ruko mat; **wahi line dobara** bolo. Galat take kat jayega, dobara wala jud jayega.
4. Har script ki shuruaat me bolo: "Script ka naam, take 1" — taaki file pehchanna asan ho.
5. File phone se yahan bhej dena (WAV/MP3/M4A — jo bhi ho, chalega).
