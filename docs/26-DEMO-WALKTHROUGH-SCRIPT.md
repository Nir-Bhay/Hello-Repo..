**Voice:** faisla baad me; script ready hai. Har hisse se pehle sambandhit composition switcher se chuno.

---

**[ShowcaseKinetic chuno · 0:00]**

“Ye pehli composition sirf ek baat saabit karti hai: pehchan. ‘Motion is the message’ — aur ye line pardah uthne ki tarah aati hai, udti hui nahi. Neeche teen rules dekho: easing, stagger, hold. Sab ek saath kabhi nahi aata; aankh ko ek samay par ek hi jagah bheja jata hai. Phir 60 ka count-up — number behta hai, ruk-ruk kar nahi badhta. Aur aakhir me video rukta hai. Khatm hone se pehle rukna bhi design hai.”

**[ShortHook chuno · ~0:22]**

“Ye dusri prayogshala dhyaan ki hai. Pehle teen second me sabse bada sawal: video cheap kyun lagta hai? Shorts me parichay ka samay nahi hota — hook pehle, sab kuchh baad me. Phir teen galtiyan, aur phir wahi teen, ab fix bankar. Jo cheez problem me dikhi thi, wahi fix me badalti hai — isiliye kahani tut ti nahi. Aur captions shabd-dar-shabd aate hain, taaki padhne ki gati bolne ki gati se mile. Aakhri frame pehle se judta hai, taaki loop kata hua na lage.”

**[DataStory chuno · ~0:42]**

“Aur ye teesri hai saboot ki: data ko jeeta hua dikhana. Pehle sawal — ‘Ek system, har hafte growth’ — phir bars spring se ugte hain, jaise unme wazan ho. Line bani hui nahi milti, banti hui dikhti hai. Aur sabse bada number, 128K, sabse aakhir me aata hai — apna rukne ka samay lekar. Teen compositions, teen hunar: pehchan, dhyaan, aur saboot. Jo production tum dekhoge, wo inhi teenon se banti hai.”
