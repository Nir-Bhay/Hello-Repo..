**Status: faisla baki hai.** Scripts tayyaar khade hain (ASI05 EN/Hinglish, P001, demo walkthrough). Awaaz ke bina Batch C ka baki kaam (SRT, mix, sound-wala v2) sach me shuru nahi ho sakta — record kisme hoga, yehi anaaj hai.

## Sifaarish kyon “apni awaaz”

Ye series ek vyakti ki zimmedari-bhari seekh hai. Machine-awaaz har jagah milti hai; **tumhari awaaz hi is channel ki woh cheez hai jo copy nahi ho sakti.** Mic aam ho to bhi chalega: band kamra, phone munh se 15–20 cm, scripts pehle se frame-time ke hisaab se likhe hain. Galti ho to line dobara — edit me jud jayega.

## Faisla = ek shabd

- `voice: own` → main recording ki ek-page guide + take-sheet bana dunga, tum padhoge, baki mera kaam.
- `voice: tts` → tum key doge, main ek chhota sample bana kar pehle sunwaunga, pasand aane par hi poori recording hogi.
