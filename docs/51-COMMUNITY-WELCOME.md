# Welcome — Pehli Baar Aane Walon ke Liye (T173)

**Hello Motion Studio** me swagat hai. Ye repo ek chhota sa animation studio hai: cybersecurity ki asal seekhein, kahaniyon me — *RACTOR* series aur real product explainers.

## Tum yahan kya kar sakte ho

- **Dekho:** landing (README se) par saari final videos aur live lab.
- **Padho:** har video ke saath walkthrough hai — `docs/17-WALKTHROUGHS-index.md` se shuru karo. ASI05 ke liye printable viewer pack bhi hai: `docs/43-ASI05-VIEWER-PACK.md`.
- **Kuch banao:** CONTRIBUTING ki "15 minute me pehla PR" wali quickstart bilkul pehle page par hai.
- **Pehla issue:** GitHub par `good first issue` label wale kaam asal aur chhote hain — unme se ek uthao, phas jao to wahi pooch lo.

## Teen usool jo yahan sab maante hain

1. **Saboot pehle, daava baad me.** "Ho gaya" tabhi jab frame/number saamne ho.
2. **Jo na ho paya, wo khula likhte hain** — "pending" shabd yahan sharm ka nahi, bharose ka hai.
3. **Sach parak kahani:** jo video kalpanik hai wo kalpanik likha milega; kisi asal company par jhootha aarop kabhi nahi.

Phas gaye? Issue kholo — sawaal bhi yogdaan hota hai.
