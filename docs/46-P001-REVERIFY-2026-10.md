P001 video ChatGPT me Google (Gmail/Drive/Calendar) connect karne ka flow dikhata hai. Product ke menu badalte rehte hain, isiliye ye jaanch: **kya video aaj bhi sahi hai?**

## Official sources se aaj ka flow

OpenAI ke apne help pages (Oct 2026 me taaza) kehte hain:

1. **Settings > Plugins** kholo, app ya use shaamil karne wala plugin chuno. ([Connected apps in ChatGPT](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11487775-connected-apps-in-chatgpt), updated Sep 2026)
2. **Install plugin** dikhe to woh chuno — isse connection/setup flow shuru ho sakta hai. ([Plugins in ChatGPT](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001256-plugins-in-chatgpt), updated 23 Oct 2026)
3. **Connect** dabao, maanga jaaye to Google account se sign in karo.
4. **Permissions padho** aur provider authorization pura karo. ([Connecting and managing app accounts](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001494-connecting-and-managing-app-accounts-in-chatgpt), updated 22 Oct 2026)
5. Google Drive ke liye: **ek hi unified Google Drive app** — Docs/Sheets/Slides ke kaam ab usi ke andar hain, alag connections nahi. ([Google Drive app and setup](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/10929079-google-drive-app-and-setup-in-chatgpt))
6. 9 Jul 2026 se **App Directory ki jagah Plugin Directory** hai; purani connections waise hi chalti rahin. ([Apps in ChatGPT](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11487775-apps-in-chatgpt))
